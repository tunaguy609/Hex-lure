# Hex-lure

Parametric OpenSCAD model for a hex-bodied offshore trolling lure head based on the requested dimensions and features.

## Files

- `/home/runner/work/Hex-lure/Hex-lure/models/offshore_hex_trolling_lure.scad` — main parametric lure model

## Design highlights

- 25.4 mm long rounded-hex head with a 24 mm maximum diameter
- Slanted nose so the top lip runs slightly longer than the bottom edge
- 18 mm diameter, 5 mm deep cupped face
- 24 mm long skirt collar with two 12 mm ramps and a 19 mm maximum outside diameter
- 16.25 mm skirt pocket extending through the collar and 20 mm into the head
- 2.2 mm centered leader bore from the nose to the start of the skirt pocket
- Four face-entry jets exiting behind the eye pockets
- Two 8 mm diameter eye recesses at 2.5 mm depth
